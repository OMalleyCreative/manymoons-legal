Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add someone's birthday?

From the main screen, tap the add button and enter their name, birthdate, and (optionally) a photo and color tag.

How does sharing work?

Select the people you'd like to share, generate a code, and send it to the other person however you like (text, email, etc.). They can enter that code in the app to import those people into their own list. Codes are single-use and expire after 24 hours.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

No — everything you add is stored only on your device. If you get a new phone, it won't transfer automatically unless you use the share feature to move people over.

How do I delete a person or all my data?

You can delete any individual person from within the app. To remove all data at once, uninstall the app — since everything is stored locally, this permanently deletes it.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

See our Privacy Policy for full details on what data is collected and how it's used.
