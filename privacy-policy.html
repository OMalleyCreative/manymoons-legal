Many Moons — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Many Moons ("the app", "we", "our") is developed by O'Malley Creative, Inc. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices — written in plain language, matching exactly what the app actually does.

Summary

  • Many Moons does not require an account, login, email address, or password.
  • The people, birthdates, and photos you add are stored only on your device.
  • The only information that ever leaves your device is what you explicitly choose to send when you use the "Share Members" feature — and even then, only names, birthdates, a color tag, and a family/friend flag are sent. Photos are never uploaded.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Information Stored Only on Your Device

When you add a person to Many Moons, the following is saved locally on your phone, in the app's private on-device database:

  • Name
  • Birthdate
  • A color you choose to tag them with
  • Whether you've marked them as family or a friend
  • A profile photo, if you choose to add one (selected from your photo library)

This information stays on your device. It is not automatically uploaded, backed up to our servers, or visible to us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted along with it.

Information Sent When You Use "Share Members"

Many Moons includes an optional feature that lets you generate a one-time code to share selected people with someone else (for example, sharing a family member's birthday with a partner so it appears in their app too).

When you choose to share people using this feature, the following is sent to our backend service (Supabase):

  • The name, birthdate, color tag, and family/friend flag of each person you selected to share
  • A randomly generated share code
  • An expiration time (24 hours from creation)

Photos are never included in a share — they are excluded automatically and never leave your device.

The share code is single-use: once someone uses it to import the shared people, it's marked as used and can't be used again. Share codes also expire automatically 24 hours after they're created. We do not currently run an automated process to permanently delete expired or used share records from our database, though they can no longer be accessed through the app once expired or used. If you'd like a share record deleted sooner, contact us using the information below.

What We Don't Collect

  • No account creation, sign-in, or authentication of any kind
  • No email address, phone number, or password
  • No device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or precise/coarse location
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No push notification tokens are collected or transmitted — reminders are scheduled entirely on your device

Third-Party Service Providers

We use Supabase solely to temporarily store share-code data described above. Supabase acts as our database host and does not have independent access to use your data for its own purposes. You can review Supabase's own privacy practices at supabase.com/privacy.

Information About Other People (Family & Friends)

Many Moons is designed to help you keep track of birthdays for people you know, which may include children (for example, tagging a person as "family"). Please only add or share information about another person — including a minor — that you have the right and appropriate authority to store and share (for example, as their parent, guardian, or with their knowledge and consent). If you are a parent or guardian and would like information about a child removed from a share record, contact us using the information below.

Your Choices

  • You can delete any person from within the app at any time, which removes them from your local device storage.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
  • You can choose not to use the "Share Members" feature at all — nothing is sent anywhere unless you use it.
  • You can request deletion of a specific share record by contacting us with the share code and approximate date.

Children's Privacy

Many Moons is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children under 13 as app users. The app may store birthdate information about a user's family members, which can include children, entered at the discretion and responsibility of the adult using the app, as described above.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if we add new features that change what data is collected — we'll update the date at the top of this page.
